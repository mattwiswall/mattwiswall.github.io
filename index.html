I am an applied microeconomist working on child development, education, and applied econometric methods. My research has been funded by the National Science Foundation, the U.S. Department of Education, the William T. Grant Foundation, and the Smith Richardson Foundation. I previously held faculty positions at Johns Hopkins University, the University of Wisconsin–Madison, and Arizona State University, and served as an Associate Editor of Econometrica and Quantitative Economics.
